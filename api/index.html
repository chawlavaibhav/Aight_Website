01The address

Send your requests here. This is the only address you need.

Base address https://api.getaight.ai/v1

The Aight API is OpenAI-compatible. That means it speaks the same language as OpenAI's own API: the same paths, the same request shape, the same reply shape. So any tool that already talks to OpenAI talks to us after you change two things — the base address and the key. The official OpenAI libraries for Python and Node, LangChain, LlamaIndex, Cursor, and plain curl all work this way.

These are the paths open to you today.

Path What it does
POST /v1/chat/completions Send messages to a model and read its reply. This is the one almost everybody uses.
POST /v1/embeddings Turn text into numbers for search and matching.
GET /v1/models List the models your key is allowed to call. Send your key with it.

Ask GET /v1/models rather than guessing a model name. The list depends on your key, and it grows. Today it includes gpt-5-mini, gemini-2.5-flash, gpt-4.1-mini, gpt-5.4-mini, gemini-2.5-pro, gemini-3.5-flash, gemini-3.1-pro-preview and text-embedding-3-small.

02Your first call

Both examples below do the same thing: send one sentence to a model and print the reply. Both read the key from an environment variable instead of hard-coding it in the text — that's how to keep it out of your code. Put your key there first — every Aight key starts with sk-aight-: 

export AIGHT_API_KEY="sk-aight-..."

curl

curl https://api.getaight.ai/v1/chat/completions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $AIGHT_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"model": "gpt-5.4-mini",
       "messages": [{"role": "user", "content": "Say hello in Hindi."}]}'

Python

import os
from openai import OpenAI

client = OpenAI(base_url="https://api.getaight.ai/v1",
                api_key=os.environ["AIGHT_API_KEY"])
reply = client.chat.completions.create(
    model="gpt-5.4-mini",
    messages=[{"role": "user", "content": "Say hello in Hindi."}],
)
print(reply.choices[0].message.content)

That is the whole change. If you already have working OpenAI code, set base_url to https://api.getaight.ai/v1 and api_key to your Aight key, and run it.

Keep the key out of your code. A key in a file that reaches GitHub is a key somebody else is spending. Tell us the moment you think a key has leaked and we freeze it.

03Getting a key

Two steps, and the first one is self-serve.

  1. Create an account at console.getaight.ai. It asks for your work email, your business name, your state and your GSTIN. We approve new accounts within 24 hours, weekends included. Nothing can be spent before that.
  2. We issue the key. Keys are still made by hand: once your account is approved and your first top-up is credited, email hello@getaight.ai with one line about what you are building, and we reply within 24 hours with your key and the models it can call. Making and revoking keys from the console yourself is not built yet.

04Two rules before your first call

Neither of these is a surprise later. They decide whether your key works at all, so they are here and not in the small print.

  • We approve every new account before it can pay. Approval happens within 24 hours of your email, weekends included. Until then the key does not work.
  • You pay in rupees before you use anything. Your key works while the balance lasts and stops the moment it runs out. There is no card on file, no monthly bill and no debt.

That's four of the eight pricing rules — minimum top-up ₹500, no expiry on your balance, unused balance refunded within 7 working days, one GST invoice per top-up. Read all eight before you email us. Models are priced in dollars, so your balance is held in dollars and each top-up converts at that day's rate, printed on the invoice.

A worked number, so nobody has to guess. Pay by UPI or bank transfer and a ₹10,000 top-up costs you ₹11,800: ₹10,000 of credit plus 18% GST. The invoice is domestic, in your own business name and GSTIN, so your finance team can claim the GST back.

05When a call fails

Write your software to branch on the HTTP status. The status is the part that does not move. The English sentence in the body is for a human reading a log: we may improve its words at any time, so do not match on them.

Where a refusal is ours rather than the model provider's, the error body also carries a short machine-readable name in its param field, listed in the Code column below. Everything in this table is what the gateway returns today, not what it will return one day.

HTTP Code What happened What to do
401 — You sent no key, or a key we do not recognise. Check the Authorization: Bearer header carries your Aight key, whole and unquoted. Keys start with sk-aight-. No key yet? Section 03 above.
402 out_of_balance Your balance is finished. Every request is refused until we credit your next top-up. The body reads: “Out of balance. Top up at console.getaight.ai. Do not retry this request automatically; it will fail until you top up.” Top up. Do not retry automatically — it will keep failing until the money lands. Already paid? Reply to your last email with the UTR.
403 key_cap_reached This one key has spent its own cap. Your account balance may be untouched; the cap is a limit you or we set on the key. The body names the cap in dollars. Use another key, or write to us to raise the cap. Do not retry: it will refuse until the cap moves.
403 — Your key is not allowed to do this. Most often the model you named is not on your key's list. Call GET /v1/models and use a name from that list, or write to us and we add the model to your key.
429 — You sent more requests in one minute than your key allows. A 429 is never about money. Wait the number of seconds in the Retry-After header, then send it again. Write to us if you need a higher limit.
503 balance_unverifiable We could not check your balance, so we refused the request rather than guess. It was not run and you were not charged. Try again in a few seconds.

Never retry a 402 automatically. A money refusal always arrives as a 402 and never as a 429, so your retry logic can tell the two apart. A 429 means slow down and try again. A 402 means stop and top up: a retry loop on a 402 only burns your own machine.

403 also covers a key you have frozen, a key past its expiry date, and an account we have put on hold. The sentence in the body says which one it is, and names the number or the date.

06When something breaks

Write to hello@getaight.ai. We read that inbox from 9 am to 9 pm IST, seven days a week. A founder replies — not a ticket queue.

Three things in the email get you an answer in one reply instead of three.

  • The HTTP status and the sentence from the error body, copied exactly.
  • The date and time of the failed request, with the time zone.
  • The model name you asked for.

That is enough for us to find the request in the log. Never send us the key itself. If you think a key has leaked, say so in the first line and we freeze it before we read the rest.
