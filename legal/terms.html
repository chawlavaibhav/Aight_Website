02What Aight is

Aight is a gateway. One key and one balance give you access to AI models from several companies.

A "model" is an AI system that reads your text and writes a reply. GPT, Gemini and Claude are models. OpenAI, Google and Anthropic make the models. Microsoft, Google and Amazon host them in their data centres. We call Microsoft, Google and Amazon our cloud providers.

An "API" is the door your software uses to talk to ours. An "API key" is a secret code your software sends with each request. It tells us which account to charge. Your "console" is the web page where you manage keys and see spend.

A "token" is a piece of text, about four characters long. Models are priced per token.

There is nothing to deliver. Credits appear in your account within minutes of a successful payment. You use them by sending requests through the API.

03Your account

  1. Aight is for businesses and professionals. You must be 18 or older to open an account.
  2. Give us accurate details: your name, business name, email, and GSTIN if you have one.
  3. The account belongs to the business, not the person who signed up. The business can name a second login for finance. Email us and we set it up.
  4. We review every new account and approve it within 24 hours of sign-up. That means 24 calendar hours, weekends included. You cannot top up or send requests before approval.
  5. We check that the account is a real business or professional with a reachable contact. We decline anonymous sign-ups, resellers of our access, and uses on our banned list (section 08). If we decline, we tell you within 24 hours and charge nothing.
  6. Keep your API keys secret. Anything sent with your key is charged to your balance, even if you did not send it.
  7. If a key leaks, revoke it in your console at once and tell us. "Revoke" means switch the key off for good. We then help you rotate it. "Rotate" means issue a fresh key to replace the old one.

04Prepaid credits

  1. Aight is prepaid only. You add money first. We deduct the cost of each request as it happens.
  2. The minimum top-up is ₹500. Your first top-up is capped at ₹2,000. You can make a second top-up 48 hours after the first. After that there is no set cap. If we ever need to limit an account, we email you first.
  3. Your balance never expires. It stays in your account until you use it or ask for a refund.
  4. Your balance is kept in US dollars, because the models are priced in dollars. Each top-up converts at that day's rate, shown on the invoice. Usage is deducted in dollars at the published model price. So two top-ups at two rates simply add up. Requests do not care which top-up they came from.
  5. Every model's rupee price is shown before you buy. Email us and we send the current price sheet the same day. We can change prices, but we email you at least 7 days before a rise.
  6. Your balance is not a bank deposit. It earns no interest and cannot be moved to another account.
  7. If your balance reaches zero, requests are refused until you top up. We never let you go into debt.

05How you pay, and the fee

  1. Payments go through Razorpay, our payment provider. Razorpay is the company that takes your money and passes it to us. You can pay by card, UPI or net banking.
  2. For top-ups above ₹20,000 you can also pay by NEFT or bank transfer. Email us for the details.
  3. Your bank or card statement shows "Ashva Intelligence Pvt Ltd", not "Aight".
  4. Every top-up carries a fixed payment-processing fee of 2%. It covers what Razorpay charges us. We show it as a separate line at checkout, before you pay.

Example. You top up ₹10,000 of usage.

Fee at 2%: ₹200. You pay ₹10,200. Your balance shows ₹10,000 worth of credit.

The fee is not refundable. See section 07.

06Invoices and GST

  1. We issue a GST tax invoice for every top-up. It uses the business name and GSTIN on your account. Check both before you pay.
  2. GST is charged at the rate the law sets for this service, currently 18%. The invoice shows it as its own line.
  3. Give us your GSTIN before you top up if you want to claim input tax credit. Input tax credit means setting the GST you paid us against the GST you collect. We cannot re-issue an invoice later.
  4. We email every invoice to your account address. Need a copy again? Email us and we send it the same working day.
  5. Need a proforma invoice before you pay? Email us and we send it the same working day. A proforma invoice is a quote laid out like an invoice, so your finance team can approve it.

07Refunds and cancellation

You can ask for a refund of your unused balance at any time. We pay it within 7 working days of your request, to the payment method you used. The 2% payment-processing fee is not refunded. Credits already used are not refunded.

During a partial refund, your keys keep working on the balance that remains. After a full refund your balance is zero, so your keys simply refuse requests. We do not delete them.

The full rules, including how to ask, are in our Refund and Cancellation Policy. That policy is part of these terms.

You own what you send and what the model returns, as far as the law and the providers allow. We claim no rights over it. We never use your prompts to train models.
